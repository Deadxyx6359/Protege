## Initial board

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: ONE polished logo exploration board comparing four DIFFERENT pixel-art logo concepts for the personal desktop AI application named "Akira". This is a concept presentation, not a final installed app asset.
Create a landscape 2 by 2 design board with generous whitespace, crisp graphic design, professional Apple-like restraint, warm near-white background (#f1f0ed), dark charcoal typography, subtle separators. Title at top "Akira" and small subtitle "Pixel identity studies". Each quadrant has a small number and concept name, a large isolated PIXEL ART emblem, a clean modern sans-serif wordmark "Akira", and two SMALL app-icon trials of that SAME emblem, one on a dark rounded square and one on a pale rounded square. All marks need strong readable silhouettes and should feel plausible at 32px. Pixel art emblems have strict square pixels, deliberately stepped edges, a restrained 4–6 color palette each, no antialiased vector diagonals, no painterly texture, no 3D renders. Text and board typography are smooth, refined modern sans-serif, while the emblems alone are pixel art. Treat each emblem as a designed identity, not a scene or generic sprite.

Top left "01  Relay A": the strongest, simplest brand mark. A stepped uppercase A built from two precise white/ice-blue electrical contact paths, a charcoal negative-space triangular opening, a mint-cyan crossbar with ONE tiny amber switching/contact pixel. The form should subtly resemble connected relay contacts while being unmistakably an A. Draw large main emblem in dark slate and teal so it reads on pale board; dark icon trial uses ice white and cyan. Modest pixel count, engineering heritage, elegant, no random circuit board traces.

Top right "02  Lumen Moth": an exquisite compact symmetrical pixel moth, four wing lobes forming a clean memorable silhouette; indigo and periwinkle outer wings, luminous aqua inner accents, one tiny warm ivory body. Very restrained speckles, 24x24-grid sensibility. Quiet and intelligent, nocturnal, grown-up, neither cute cartoon nor scary creature. Not the usual AI four-point sparkle.

Bottom left "03  Quiet Beacon": a tiny luminous lantern with a squared arch handle and simple enclosing stepped frame, pale cyan glass and a single warm-ivory core, dark desaturated blue outline, one muted violet shadow. Beautiful negative space around the glass. Front view, an icon of guidance and a calm daily companion. No landscape, no ornate Japanese ornament, no gradients required to recognize it.

Bottom right "04  Orbit Seed": a compact seed-shaped dark teal world with a slim asymmetric pale mint pixel orbit, a tiny two-leaf shoot emerging from its upper edge, an ice-blue highlight. Organic and cosmic in one emblem, strong compact silhouette; sophisticated and slightly mysterious, not a cartoon smiling planet. Deep teal, sage, ice blue, charcoal. Keep it simpler than a game inventory item.

Do not imitate the AKIRA film wordmark or any existing technology logo. No red cyberpunk typography, faces, robots, brains, shields, padlocks, decorative surrounding stars, stock mockup objects, extra captions, fake kanji, or busy backgrounds. Exact text only: "Akira", "Pixel identity studies", "01  Relay A", "02  Lumen Moth", "03  Quiet Beacon", "04  Orbit Seed". Equal visual weight and considered spacing across all four concepts. The mini icon trials must match the main concept faithfully. The board should look like a thoughtful brand designer's first presentation, with pixel artwork that is sharp and intentional.
